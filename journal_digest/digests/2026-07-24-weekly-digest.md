### Imaging of nanoscale polar textures in quantum paraelectric SrTiO<sub>3</sub>
**Nature** | Yang Zhang, Suk Hyun Sung, Nishkarsh Agarwal, Maya Gates, Cong Li et al. (+3 more)  
*Published: 22 Jul 2026* | ⚪ Routine  
[Full paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10823-x)

> This study utilized cryo-STEM imaging to observe nanoscale polar textures in strontium titanate (SrTiO3). They discovered that below 105 K, SrTiO3 forms periodic polar nanodomains, which then fragment into disordered structures at even lower temperatures (below 40 K). This observation links quantum paraelectricity to the nanoscale ordering and subsequent disordering of polar textures.
