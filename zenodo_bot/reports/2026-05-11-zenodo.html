scRNA-seq background RNA

AI Summary: This dataset provides 10x Genomics single-cell RNA sequencing data, including genotype-based demultiplexing information and CellRanger BAM files. The data originates from a mixture of mouse kidney cells from three different mouse strains: BL6, SVLMJ, and CAST. It was generated using 10x experiments and includes intermediate files from genotype analysis. This resource is designed for benchmarking methods aimed at estimating and removing background RNA in single-cell data, making it valuable for single-cell and kidney research.
