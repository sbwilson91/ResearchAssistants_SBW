That 1168 m of elevation across the week explains the humbling experience: hills are a documented weakness, and the Bornholm terrain tested that directly. The pacing was disciplined — HR 130 on a 67.9 km effort is close to ideal damage-minimisation — but the elevation load means the muscular cost was higher than a flat ultra of equivalent distance would produce. The other significant session was Monday's Copenhagen Running effort (25 Aug): 5.1 km with a single interval peaking at 3:15/km, functioning as a pre-ultra prime. That was smart programming — enough to keep the legs turned over without digging into reserves before the main event. Total week at 85 km is substantially above the rolling 8-week average of 67.1 km, which tells the true story: this wasn't a taper week, it was an ultra week with a quality primer attached.
