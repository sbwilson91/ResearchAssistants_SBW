> This research aimed to develop a safer way to target PD-L1, a molecule found on cancer cells and immune cells that can suppress anti-tumor responses. The scientists created a new type of T-cell therapy using a "chimeric switch receptor" (CSR) that specifically targets PD-L1. > This study investigates how the primate cerebral cortex expanded by examining the development of neural progenitors.
