### [Coupled transcriptomic divergence establishes a human-specific synaptic glial precursor state](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.19.745872)

> This study investigated how the human brain evolved its unique complexity compared to other mammals. Researchers compared gene activity during brain development in humans, macaques, mice, and ferrets using single-cell analysis. They discovered that genes changing during evolution tend to shift their timing and role in cell development together. Specifically, a network of genes related to synapses was found to be uniquely active in early human oligodendrocyte precursor cells, a state not seen in other primates. These specialized human cells then communicate with neighboring developing neurons, suggesting this coordinated change in development is a key driver of human brain evolution.

Researchers infected brain organoids with HCMV and treated them with four different antiviral drugs: aciclovir, ganciclovir, letermovir, and maribavir. They found that letermovir was the most effective at reducing viral RNA, followed by ganciclovir, maribavir, and then aciclovir. > This study investigated why nanomedicines that work in lab dishes often fail in patients, specifically focusing on how nanoparticles change in the tumor environment. Researchers used patient-derived organoids, which better mimic tumors than standard cell cultures, to study how graphene and silicon quantum dots interact with cells. ### [AutoBlot: deterministic single-cell western blotting reveals proteomic diversity in rare cell populations](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.22.733890)
**Deroy, C.; Islam, M. N.; Goldhammer, N.; Jiang, L.; Rosenbluth, J. M.; Herr, A. E.** | Bioengineering | 2026-09-03 | `organoid`

> This research addresses the challenge of accurately measuring protein levels in individual cells, especially when starting material is limited. The scientists developed a new method called AutoBlot, which uses precise dispensing guided by imaging to place single cells into specific wells for analysis. This technique significantly improved the success rate of loading single cells compared to previous methods. Applying AutoBlot to breast organoid cells, they discovered variations in protein levels related to the donor, BRCA1 status, and menopausal status, and also identified different forms of a key protein. This work demonstrates that precisely loading single cells is a valuable strategy for detailed protein analysis in complex biological samples.

## Prostate

### [Pharmacologic Targeting of ZNF281 Suppresses Metastatic Prostate Cancer Beyond Androgen Receptor Dependence](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.03.749302)
**Huang, G.; Lorenzana Carrillo, M. A.; Chen, H.; Chen, R.; Abbasi Dezfouli, S.; Nanoa, J.; Rimawi, D.; Haromy, A. S.; Zha...** | Cancer Biology | 2026-09-07 | `organoid`

> This study investigated the role of the protein ZNF281 in prostate cancer, particularly its metastatic form. Researchers found that ZNF281 drives cancer growth by both enhancing the activity of the androgen receptor (AR) and by activating separate pathways that promote tumor growth and spread. They developed a new drug, an orally available molecule called ZIM, that blocks ZNF281's function. This ZIM drug, along with removing ZNF281, effectively reduced prostate cancer growth and metastasis in animal models without causing harm, and it proved more effective than an existing treatment in lab-grown human cancer samples.
