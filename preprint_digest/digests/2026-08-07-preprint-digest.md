### [An integrated human forebrain organoid reveals microglia-mediated CD8⁺ T cell recruitment and neuroimmune dysfunction in Alzheimer's disease pathology](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.25.727443)
**MA, S.** | Bioengineering | 2026-08-06 | `organoid`

> This research investigates how the human immune system contributes to Alzheimer's disease by creating a model of the human brain. Scientists used a special organoid system that included brain cells, immune cells called microglia, and T cells to mimic Alzheimer's pathology. They found that microglia help clear amyloid plaques and support neuron development, but also trigger inflammation and attract T cells, creating a harmful cycle. Importantly, blocking certain pathways involved in this T cell recruitment also affected microglia's behavior and could be a potential therapeutic strategy. This new organoid system offers a way to study brain-immune interactions and test new treatments for neuroinflammatory diseases like Alzheimer's.
