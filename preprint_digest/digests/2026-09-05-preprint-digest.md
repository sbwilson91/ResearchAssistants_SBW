### [Antiviral activity versus host response: contrasting transcriptomic footprints of four antivirals in human cytomegalovirus-infected brain organoids](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.01.722178)
**Egilmezer, E.; Rawlinson, W.; Foster, C. S. P.** | Microbiology | 2026-09-03 | `organoid`

> This study investigated how different antiviral drugs affect human cytomegalovirus (HCMV) infection in a model of the human brain. Researchers infected brain organoids with HCMV and treated them with four different antiviral drugs: aciclovir, ganciclovir, letermovir, and maribavir. They found that letermovir was the most effective at reducing viral RNA, followed by ganciclovir, maribavir, and then aciclovir. While all drugs reduced viral load, letermovir significantly altered host gene expression related to metabolism, whereas other drugs had less impact on the host's genetic activity. This research helps understand which antivirals might be best for treating HCMV in the brain by examining both viral reduction and host gene restoration.
