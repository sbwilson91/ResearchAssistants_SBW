> This study investigated whether zebrafish spinal cord regeneration after injury uses the same processes as normal development or employs unique mechanisms. Researchers used single-cell RNA sequencing and in vivo experiments to identify a specific "injury-induced ERG" (iiERG) cell state in larval zebrafish. They discovered that this iiERG state, characterized by stemness factors like lin28a, appears to preserve stem cells rather than directly generating neurons.
