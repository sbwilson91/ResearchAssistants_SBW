### [Evaluation design shapes estimates of cross-donor generalization in single-cell classification of cell-type](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.26.761949)
**Chen, X.; Li, J.; Battle, A.** | Bioinformatics | 2026-11-10 | `scRNA`

> This study investigated how different ways of testing cell-type classifiers affect their ability to generalize to new individuals. The researchers compared using random cell splits versus holding out entire donors for testing. They found that random cell splits consistently overestimated classifier performance, especially for more complex datasets. This overestimation was not resolved by accounting for technical variations or using advanced statistical models. The findings highlight that testing with held-out donors is crucial for accurately assessing how well a classifier will work on new, unseen individuals, particularly when the classification task is challenging.
