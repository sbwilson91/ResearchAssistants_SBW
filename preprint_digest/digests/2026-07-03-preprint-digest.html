This research investigates how a specific mutation in the TDP-43 protein, linked to ALS and FTD, leads to neurodegeneration. Scientists created three-dimensional human brain organoids with this mutation and observed increased TDP-43 abnormalities, RNA processing errors, and signs of cell death.
