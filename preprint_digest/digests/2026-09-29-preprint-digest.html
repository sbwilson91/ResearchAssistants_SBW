This study investigates how accurately cell types in lab-grown kidney organoids match those in real kidneys. Researchers developed new ways to measure the reliability of cell-type labels assigned by computer algorithms, by testing how stable the labels are under simulated errors and how well the algorithm's predictions align with the assigned labels. This work is important because it helps assess the quality of kidney organoids for research and medical applications.
