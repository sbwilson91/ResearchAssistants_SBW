**Chen, X.; Li, J.; Battle, A.** | Bioinformatics | 2026-11-10 | `scRNA`

> This study investigated how different ways of testing cell-type classifiers affect their ability to generalize to new individuals. Researchers found that randomly splitting cells for testing, instead of holding out entire donors, consistently overestimated classifier performance. This overestimation was observed even after accounting for technical variations and using advanced statistical models. They discovered that while the brain cells tried to fight off viruses, this response didn't help the disease.
