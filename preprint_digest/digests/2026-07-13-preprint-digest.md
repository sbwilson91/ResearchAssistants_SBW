> This research aimed to develop more accurate and faster lab-grown models of human liver cells for studying diseases and testing drugs. Scientists used a 10-day process to create two types of liver models from stem cells: individual liver-like cells (HLCs) and more complex liver organoids (HLOs). The study found that both models showed signs of mature liver function, but the HLOs, which included various liver cell types, better mimicked the natural liver environment. This new platform, especially the 3D organoids, offers a quicker and more realistic way to study liver health and disease.
