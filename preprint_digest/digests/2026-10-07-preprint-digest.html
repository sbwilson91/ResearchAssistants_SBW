This study investigated how to non-invasively study early human brain development, specifically focusing on the rapid changes in cell structure. Researchers used advanced MRI techniques on human brain organoids, which are lab-grown models of brain tissue, to examine water movement within them. They found that the MRI measurements were consistent in organoids grown with a specific developmental plan but more varied in those grown without, mirroring differences in their internal structure.
