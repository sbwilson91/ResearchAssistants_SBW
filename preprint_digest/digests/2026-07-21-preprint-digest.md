> This study investigated why most rectal cancer patients don't fully respond to neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy (CRT). Researchers analyzed the cellular responses of patient-derived organoids to CRT, using various molecular profiling techniques. They discovered that a cancer stem cell's "fate" or ability to adapt, rather than just DNA damage, dictates treatment response.
