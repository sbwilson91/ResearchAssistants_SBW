> This study investigated how different ways of measuring "neural complexity" relate to the actual structure of brain networks. Researchers used computer simulations of brain activity, systematically changing the connections between simulated neurons to create various network structures. They found that measures of complexity based on observing spontaneous brain activity were inconsistent, with some reflecting network structure better than others.
