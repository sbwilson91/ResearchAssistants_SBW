This study investigated whether transplanting human retinal organoids could help protect and restore vision after optic nerve injury. Researchers transplanted these organoids into mice with crushed optic nerves and observed the effects over several weeks. They found that the transplants helped preserve the structure of the retina and reduced the loss of retinal ganglion cells and their connections.
