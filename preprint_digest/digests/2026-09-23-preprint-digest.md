## Brain

### [Oncohistone inhibition reshapes tumor-microenvironment communication in Diffuse Midline Glioma (DMG)](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.17.731637)
**Khairkhah, N.; Ibrahim, M. M. H.; Galban, S. L.; Faunce, M.; Rober, L.; Baker, C.; Doherty, R.; Cartaxo, R.; Koschmann, ...** | Cancer Biology | 2026-09-16 | `CRISPR`

> This study investigated the role of the H3K27M oncohistone in established diffuse midline gliomas (DMGs), particularly its impact on tumor growth and the surrounding tumor microenvironment. Researchers created models where the H3K27M oncohistone could be turned on and off to observe its effects. They found that H3K27M expression significantly alters the tumor's cellular characteristics and the tumor microenvironment. Specifically, the oncohistone promotes interactions between tumor cells and neurons, leading to increased neuronal activity and connectivity that fuels tumor growth, partly through enhanced glutamatergic signaling. This work sheds light on how H3K27M influences established tumors and their microenvironment, suggesting potential therapeutic targets related to neuronal signaling.

## Bone

### [Spatially resolved gene expression and tumour-microenvironment interactions across malignant transformation in IDH-mutant glioma](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.08.749421)
**Ivanova, A.; Ayyadhury, S.; Munoz, D. G.; Hopkins, M.; Aboualizadeh, F.; Spears, M.; Wu, M.; Pugh, T.; Verhaak, R. G. W....** | Cancer Biology | 2026-09-16 | `spatial transcriptomics`

> This study investigated how IDH-mutant gliomas, common in young adults, progress from less aggressive to more malignant forms. Researchers used advanced spatial transcriptomics on tumor samples from two patients at different stages of the disease to understand the underlying gene activity. They found that while tumors became more aggressive, the rate of cell division at the tumor's edge actually decreased, and genes related to invasion became more active. This suggests that traditional bulk analysis methods might miss crucial spatial changes driving tumor progression, highlighting the need for new ways to measure these evolving patterns.
