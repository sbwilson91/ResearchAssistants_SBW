> This study investigated how the gene ST3GAL3 influences the balance between excitatory and inhibitory signals in brain synapses, which is often disrupted in neurodevelopmental disorders. Researchers used human stem cells, creating versions with and without the ST3GAL3 gene, and then differentiated them into brain cells. They found that cells lacking ST3GAL3 showed abnormal electrical activity patterns, with longer and more variable bursts of signals.
