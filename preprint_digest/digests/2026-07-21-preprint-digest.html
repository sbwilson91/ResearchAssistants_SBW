This study investigated how microgravity, a stressor in spaceflight, might affect brain cells vulnerable to Parkinson's disease. Researchers exposed human brain organoids and neurons to simulated microgravity for three days. They found that this exposure damaged the cells, reduced their "Parkinson's disease-like" characteristics, and impaired their mitochondria and protein production. This study investigates how dense biological tissues, like epithelial tissues, manage internal stress to maintain stability. Researchers used force measurements and stress inference to observe cell division in these tissues. They found that instead of dividing along their longest axis as previously thought (Hertwig's rule), cells in dense tissues divide along the direction of greatest stress.
