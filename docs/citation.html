Cross-modal transfer enables zero-shot prediction of drug responses across cell types

Cheng Luo, Miguel Hu, Yahong Wu et al. | 2026-11-01

AI Summary: This study addresses the challenge of predicting how drugs affect gene expression in new cellular contexts without needing direct drug measurements. Researchers developed GRAFT, a framework that transfers information from genetic perturbation responses to infer chemical (drug)-induced transcriptional changes, combined with cross-context prediction. GRAFT significantly improved the accuracy of drug response predictions—including correlation, directional agreement, and magnitude—outperforming other methods, particularly for drugs with context-dependent effects. The framework was used to create GRAFT-Atlas, a large dataset of predictions across multiple cell types, which successfully revealed pharmacological relationships, shared biological programs, and context-dependent transcriptional changes.
