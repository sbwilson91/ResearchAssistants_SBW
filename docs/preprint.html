This study investigated how problems with primary cilia, which are important for cell signaling during development, affect human brain development, specifically the cerebellum. Researchers used lab-grown human cerebellar organoids with genetic mutations linked to a disorder called Joubert syndrome. They found that these mutated organoids had fewer specialized nerve cells called Purkinje cells, along with problems in how new nerve cells were made and too much cell division.
