This study investigated how having an extra copy of chromosome 21 (trisomy 21), which causes Down syndrome, affects early brain development. Researchers used lab-grown brain organoids derived from cells of individuals with and without Down syndrome to compare their development. They found that trisomy 21 organoids showed delayed growth, fewer and less mature oligodendrocytes (cells that form myelin), and reduced myelination, along with more astrocytes and slower neuronal maturation. G2T: Tissue Reconstruction from Gene Expression via Embedding-Distance Flow Matching

Birk, S.; Theis, F. J.; Lotfollahi, M. | Genomics | 2026-08-28 | scRNA

This research addresses the challenge of reconstructing the spatial organization of cells within a tissue when only their gene expression data is available, a common limitation of single-cell RNA sequencing. The scientists developed a deep learning model called G2T, which uses an attention-based Transformer to predict the distances between cells based solely on their gene expression. G2T was found to outperform existing methods in accurately reconstructing spatial relationships, as demonstrated on a benchmark dataset of mouse brain tissue. This work offers a promising new approach to inferring tissue architecture from gene expression data, which could be valuable for understanding cellular interactions and tissue organization.
