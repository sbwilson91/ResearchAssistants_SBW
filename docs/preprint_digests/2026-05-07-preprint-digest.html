Chang, Y.; Yu, X.; Ahmed, T.; Zhao, Y.; He, S.; Ye, X. | Pathology | 2026-05-01 | spatial transcriptomics

This study aimed to better understand the spatial organization of vessel-wall abnormalities in Moyamoya disease (MMD), a condition characterized by progressive arterial narrowing and abnormal vessel formation. Researchers used advanced spatial transcriptomics and immunofluorescence on superficial temporal artery samples from MMD patients, complemented by gain- and loss-of-function experiments in human brain endothelial cells. They discovered that MMD vascular tissue showed altered cellular architecture, increased extracellular matrix components, and a significant upregulation of sulfatase 1 (SULF1). In cell models, SULF1 was found to enhance key angiogenic processes like cell migration, tube formation, and associated signaling pathways, with these effects being attenuated by heparinase III.
