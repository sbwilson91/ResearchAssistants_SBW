## Intestine

### [Development of gemcitabine-modified miR-15a as a novel, multimodal treatment strategy to overcome 5-FU and oxaliplatin resistance in colorectal cancer](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.25.720825)
**OJHA, A.; Pal, A.; Chao, M.; Davuluri, R. V.; Ju, J.** | Cancer Biology | 2026-04-29 | `organoid`

> Colorectal cancer (CRC) frequently develops resistance to 5-fluorouracil (5-FU) chemotherapy, a major clinical hurdle, while promising microRNA-based therapies face challenges with stability and delivery. To overcome these issues, researchers designed Gem-miR-15a, a novel molecule that integrates the tumor-suppressing activity of microRNA-15a with the chemotherapeutic potency of gemcitabine. Its efficacy was evaluated across various preclinical models, including drug-resistant CRC cell lines, 3D spheroids, patient-derived organoids, and in vivo metastatic models. Gem-miR-15a exhibited potent anti-proliferative activity, proving 100 to 5000 times more effective than 5-FU and oxaliplatin. Importantly, it successfully retained efficacy in and overcame acquired resistance in CRC models resistant to both 5-FU and oxaliplatin, suggesting a promising new therapeutic approach for drug-resistant CRC.

## Liver

### [Allogeneic CRISPR-Engineered CAR-T Cells Drive Potent Antitumor Activity in Solid Tumors](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.25.720815)
**Huo, M.; Li, D.; Li, N.; Quan, A.; Liang, T.; Henderson, D.; Sagert, J.; Pharm, M.; Hanley, L.; Maeng, K.; Eule, M.; Ho,...** | Immunology | 2026-04-29 | `CRISPR`

> Current CAR-T cell therapies show limited success in solid tumors, partly due to issues with patient-derived T cells. To address this, researchers developed an "off-the-shelf" allogeneic CAR-T platform using CRISPR-Cas9 to precisely edit healthy donor T cells, inserting CARs targeting tumor antigens GPC2 or GPC3 while disrupting B2M. These engineered CAR-T cells demonstrated potent, antigen-specific killing of tumor cells across various models. GPC2-targeted cells showed enhanced anti-tumor activity in neuroblastoma, leading to tumor regression and prolonged survival, with repeated dosing proving effective and safe; GPC3-targeted cells also showed robust activity against liver cancer. This scalable, genome-engineered strategy offers a promising therapeutic approach, supporting the clinical development of readily available CAR-T cell therapies for a range of pediatric and adult solid tumors.

## Bone

### [Primary cilia regulate mechanical adaptation of tendon and enthesis via hedgehog signaling](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.25.720837)
**Zhang, E.; Fang, F.** | Developmental Biology | 2026-04-29 | `scRNA`

> This study investigated how primary cilia in tendons convert mechanical stimuli into structural and functional adaptations, a process crucial for tendon health and healing. Researchers re-analyzed human tendon cell gene expression data and utilized tendon-specific cilia knockout mice subjected to mechanical unloading, evaluating tissue structure, mechanical properties, and Hedgehog (Hh) signaling both *in vivo* and *in vitro*. They found that mechanical force alters cilia- and Hh-related gene expression in human tendon fibroblasts. Furthermore, deleting cilia in mice impaired force-driven remodeling of tendon structure and mechanical strength, and also led to impaired Hh signaling. These findings suggest that primary cilia are essential mechanosensors in tendons, linking mechanical stimuli to Hh signaling pathways to regulate tissue adaptation.
