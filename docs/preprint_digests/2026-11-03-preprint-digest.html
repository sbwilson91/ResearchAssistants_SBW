This study investigated how problems with primary cilia, tiny hair-like structures on cells, affect the development of the human cerebellum. Researchers used lab-grown human cerebellar organoids with genetic defects in a gene called RPGRIP1L, which is linked to a brain disorder called Joubert syndrome.
